<h1>About {BRAND}</h1>
<p>{BRAND} helps you choose an online course platform with plain-English, sourced comparisons. {BRAND} is owned and operated by Joshua Israel Ventures LLC.</p>
<h2>How we review</h2><p>We read each platform's own pricing pages, help centres and policies, cite them on every review, and date-stamp our checks. We don't copy other review sites; our writing and analysis are our own.</p>
<h2>Our method in practice</h2><ul><li><strong>Official sources first.</strong> Every price, trial length and refund term in our <a href="reviews/">platform reviews</a> and <a href="compare/">comparisons</a> links to the page it came from. If we cannot confirm a figure on an official page, we say "see official pricing" rather than guess.</li>
<li><strong>Re-checked, not set and forgotten.</strong> A script re-fetches each source and flags changed prices or trial lengths, which we then verify by hand before updating the <a href="pricing-tracker/">price and free-trial tracker</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Open data.</strong> The tracker and the <a href="subscription-calculator/">monthly vs annual calculator</a> publish their data as CSV files that anyone may reuse with a link (CC BY 4.0).</li>
<li><strong>Clear about what we haven't done.</strong> We say when we have not personally tested a feature, and our <a href="news/">news posts</a> cite the original announcements.</li>
<li><strong>Money never buys a verdict.</strong> Some links may be affiliate links (see our <a href="disclosure.html">affiliate disclosure</a>); no company pays for placement or reviews.</li></ul>
<p>Found an error or an outdated price? <a href="contact.html">Tell us</a> and we will re-check the source.</p>
