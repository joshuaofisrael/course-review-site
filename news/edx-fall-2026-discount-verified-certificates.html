edX takes 15% off certificates until November 4

By the CourseGauge editors · Published October 25, 2026

edX is running a fall promotion that takes 15% off select paid offerings until November 4, 2026. According to edX's promotion page, the discount covers select verified courses, Professional Certificates, MicroBachelors programs and MicroMasters programs, and you apply it by entering the code FALL2026 at checkout on edX.org. The offer ends at 11:59 p.m. US Eastern Time on November 4.

The fine print

edX's terms say the discount does not apply to degree programs, cannot be combined with any other discount code or offer, and can be changed or cancelled by edX at any time. It applies to select courses and programs, so check that the price drops at checkout before you rely on it. We have not tested the code ourselves.

How to get the most from it

The discount only matters if you were going to pay. Most edX courses can still be audited for free, with temporary access to the course materials but no graded assignments or certificate. A sensible approach is to audit the first part of a course, decide whether you actually want the verified certificate, and only then upgrade with the code before November 4 (and before that course's own upgrade deadline). If you are weighing edX against a subscription, our Coursera vs edX comparison covers how per-course pricing compares with Coursera Plus.
