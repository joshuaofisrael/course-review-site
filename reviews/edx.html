edX review (2026): university courses with a free audit track

Short answer: edX lets you audit many university courses free, then pay to upgrade to a verified certificate. Prices are set per course.

Key facts

Pricing modelPer course; free audit track or paid verified track
Free optionAudit track: temporary access to course materials, no graded assignments or certificate
CertificatesVerified certificate on the paid track
PriceVaries by course; check the course's enrollment options
Free trialNo trial needed: audit track is free

Who it suits

Learners who want academic content and to try a course before paying for a certificate.

Who should look elsewhere

People wanting an all-you-can-learn subscription or creative hobby classes.

Compare current prices for all platforms in our price and free-trial tracker, check whether an annual plan pays off with the monthly vs annual calculator, or take the platform quiz.

Our take

Audit first; upgrade before the course's upgrade deadline only if you want the certificate.
