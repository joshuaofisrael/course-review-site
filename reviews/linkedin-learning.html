LinkedIn Learning review (2026): business skills bundled with Premium

Short answer: LinkedIn Learning is solid for business and software skills, but individuals can only get it through LinkedIn Premium or an employer, school or library.

Key facts

Pricing modelNo standalone individual subscription; included with LinkedIn Premium. PCMag reports Premium Career at $39.99/month or $239.88/year
Free trialPremium free trial (typically one month), card required
A la carteIndividual course purchases are not offered
Catalog16,000+ courses (per LinkedIn)
CertificatesCertificate of completion

Who it suits

Job seekers who also want Premium networking features, and anyone with free access via a library or employer.

Who should look elsewhere

Learners who only want courses and do not value Premium's other features.
