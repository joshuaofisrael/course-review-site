Alison review (2026): free to finish, pay only for the certificate

By the CourseGauge editorial team

Last updated November 20, 2026

Short answer: Alison still lets you study and finish a course without paying. The certificate help article, marked updated the day before we read it on 20 Nov 2026, says you need 70% on every assessment, a free Learner Record is enough to show you finished, and a digital or parchment certificate is a separate purchase in the Shop. The article does not print the Shop prices.

Key facts

Pricing modelCourses are free to study and complete. Certificates are optional purchases. The help article points to the Shop for the amounts and does not list them.
Free optionStudy every section and sit the assessments at no charge. A Learner Record of a finished course is free. You do not have to buy a certificate to have finished.
Pass markComplete every section (a green tick on each) and score at least 70% on each assessment. That rule is in the certificate help article.
CertificatesAfter you pass, a digital certificate or a mailed parchment can be bought in the Shop. Some courses include a free digital certificate. The certificate language is the language of the final assessment. A purchased certificate carries a QR code that opens a verification page.
Paying in the appThe help article says digital certificates bought in the Alison app can be paid with Apple Pay or Google Pay only. The browser Shop also offers card, PayPal and bank transfer. Printed certificates need a full delivery address.

Who it suits

A learner who wants a structured free course and can live with a Learner Record, or who will pay the Shop price only if a specific employer has said an Alison certificate counts. The 70% rule is at least a real bar, unlike a certificate that appears for pressing play.

Who should look elsewhere

Anyone who needs a university transcript or a professional licence. Alison's certificate is Alison's. It is also a clumsy fit if you care which language is printed on the certificate and you sit the final quiz in the wrong one: the help article says you must retake the assessment in the other language and score higher than before. A parchment already posted is not reprinted. You place a new order.

The 70 percent rule

Alison's certificate article was marked "Updated yesterday" when we opened it on 20 Nov 2026. The completion rule is concrete. Every section needs a green tick, and every assessment needs a score of at least 70%. Until both are true, there is nothing in the Shop to buy.

The "is Alison really free" article still says you can study and finish without paying, and that a Learner Record can be downloaded. The certificate article repeats the point in plainer words: you do not have to purchase a certificate in order to certify. The Learner Record is the free proof. The digital file and the parchment are souvenirs you pay for.

The language trap on the certificate

The certificate language is chosen for you. It is the language you used for the final assessment. If you want a different language on the certificate, you retake that assessment in the other language, and the language updates only if the new score is higher than the old one. The digital file then switches. A parchment that has already been dispatched does not. The article says you would place a new order, and it tells you to check the language before you pay so you are not charged twice.

Check the name spelling in the Shop before you pay as well. The article walks through an Edit Name step and warns that a correction later costs extra. We are not quoting a correction fee, because the article does not state one.

How the Shop takes payment

In a browser, the article lists credit or debit card, PayPal, and bank transfer. In the app, digital certificates and digital diplomas are limited to Apple Pay or Google Pay, which the article attributes to Apple and Google rules. If you do not have those, it says to switch to the browser. Printed certificates need a delivery address, and the article suggests a postcode and a PO Box.

A bought certificate or diploma includes a QR code. Scanning it opens an Alison page that shows the credential. The transcript has a verification link too. That is useful if an employer asks whether the PDF is real. It does not make the certificate a degree. For free platforms side by side, use best free online course platforms. Our take

Finish the course free, download the Learner Record, and only then open the Shop. Do not assume a dollar amount from an old review. The help article we read refuses to state one and sends you to the Shop, where the price can move. If the goal is a free school curriculum with no certificate upsell, Khan Academy is the cleaner product. If you want a free audit and a paid university-style certificate, compare edX in best free online course platforms.
