The Great Courses Plus review (2026): $4.99 for 2 months, then $15.99

By the CourseGauge editorial team

Last updated November 21, 2026

Short answer: On 21 Nov 2026 the plan data behind The Great Courses Plus checkout returned one US membership: $4.99 a month for the first two months, then $15.99. The plans page banner said that offer ends 30 Nov 2026. The same plan says there is no refund if you cancel early. No annual US price was in that data, so this review does not repeat the euro figures from October.

Key facts

Pricing modelOne US monthly membership was in the plan data on 21 Nov 2026. It is a special offer: $4.99 a month for two months, then $15.99. The plans page banner dated the offer through 30 Nov 2026. Annual and quarterly US prices were not in the response.
PriceUS: $4.99 for each of the first two months ($9.98), then $15.99 a month. The plan's own discount figure is $11.00, which is 15.99 minus 4.99. A first year on these rates is $169.88 (9.98 plus ten months at 15.99). A later year at $15.99 is $191.88. Those yearly totals are our arithmetic, not a price the page printed as an annual plan.
What you getThe plan benefits say the library covers history, science and math, philosophy and religion, literature and language, health, hobbies, professional growth and travel, with over 20,000 lectures, unlimited streaming, and no ads. The homepage FAQ says new courses are added every month, and that individual Great Courses can be bought outright and kept.
CertificatesNot mentioned in the plan benefits or the homepage FAQ we read.
Free trialThe homepage button says Start Your FREE Trial. The only US plan in the data was the $4.99 intro, and it did not state a number of free days. We are not repeating an earlier 7-day figure.
RefundsThe plan disclaimer: no refund for early termination. Access ends when the paid billing period ends. The benefits list also says Cancel Anytime. Read together, you can stop the renewal, and you do not get the current month back.

Who it suits

Someone who wants long professor-led lectures and will still be watching in month three, when the price becomes $15.99. Two months at $4.99 is $9.98, which is a cheap way to find out whether the lecture style suits you. At our last read of MasterClass, Standard was $120 for a year. A first year of this Plus offer, on the arithmetic above, is $169.88. The catalogs are different products. The head-to-head list is MasterClass alternatives.

Who should look elsewhere

Anyone who needs a certificate, a short creative class, or a price that is still $4.99 in month six. It is also a weak fit if you wanted an annual bill: that option was not in the US plan data on 21 Nov. Career courses belong on Coursera or edX.

What the checkout data actually contained

The plans page HTML, fetched on 21 Nov 2026 without running its scripts, showed a banner and almost no prices: "LIMITED TIME OFFER: $4.99 per Month for 2 Months! Offer Ends 11/30/2026." The page then asks its own plans service for the rest. That service, plus.thegreatcourses.com/rest/V1/plans, returned five monthly offers the same day. Only one was marked for the US.

US Plus membership in the plans data, 21 Nov 2026
WindowChargeWhere it is written
Months 1 and 2$4.99 each monthPlan disclaimer and the first-payment field
Month 3 onward$15.99 a monthDisclaimer: "On the third and subsequent months, you will be charged $15.99."
Discount label$11.00The offer object. 15.99 minus 4.99 is 11.00, so this is the monthly gap, not a two-month credit.

Two months of the intro cost $9.98. Ten further months at $15.99 cost $159.90. Added together, a first year that starts on this offer is $169.88, if the rate does not change and you do not cancel. A second year with no intro is 15.99 times 12, or $191.88. None of those yearly figures is an annual plan. The response had no US annual row and no quarterly row. Australia, the EU, the UK and Canada each had their own currency version of a two-month intro. This review is about the US row.

Cancel anytime, and no refund

The benefits list says Cancel Anytime. The disclaimer, on the same plan, says there is no refund for early termination and that access ends when the paid period ends. Those sentences answer different questions. You can stop the next charge. You do not get a partial month back. There is no certificate in the benefits list, and the homepage FAQ does not describe one either.

That FAQ does draw a useful line. Plus is the streaming library, "over 20,000 lectures," always ad-free, with new courses each month. The Great Courses, the shop, sells individual courses you keep. If one history series is the whole goal, buying it is the product the FAQ is pointing at. If you want a stack of short project classes instead of lectures, the nearer pages are Skillshare and Skillshare vs Domestika.

Our take

Open the plans page before 30 Nov 2026 if the $4.99 intro is the reason you would join. After the two months, the plan you are agreeing to is $15.99, and the disclaimer says an early cancellation is not refunded. If you only want one course on your shelf, the homepage FAQ points at buying that course from The Great Courses instead of renting the library. Prices for the wider set are in the price tracker.
