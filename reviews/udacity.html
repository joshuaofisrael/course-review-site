Udacity review (2026): project-based tech Nanodegrees

Short answer: Udacity sells All Access to its whole catalog of Nanodegree programs and courses, aimed at job-ready tech skills. It is expensive at list price, so time your enrollment.

Key facts

Pricing modelAll Access subscription: $249/month, or $846 upfront for four months (15% saving), then month-to-month
DiscountsPersonalised offers and periodic sales
CertificatesNanodegree program certificates
CatalogAll Nanodegrees and courses while subscribed

Who it suits

Motivated learners who can finish a Nanodegree in a few focused months.

Who should look elsewhere

Casual or budget learners.

Our take

Use the four-month bundle only if you can commit the hours.
