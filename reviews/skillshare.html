Skillshare review (2026): project-based creative classes

Short answer: Skillshare suits creatives who like short, hands-on classes with a project at the end. The web price is an annual plan charged upfront.

Key facts

Pricing modelAnnual membership $167.88/yr (shown as $13.99/month) on the web pricing page
Monthly optionVia the Skillshare app through your app store
Free trial7 days
FeaturesOffline viewing, subtitles, transcripts
CertificatesNot credential-focused
Regional pricingPrices vary by location
Refunds7-day trial: one-time refund if requested within 48 hours of the charge and unused; no refunds for renewals; EU/UK 14-day cooling-off

Who it suits

Illustrators, designers, photographers, crafters and freelancers who learn best by making something.

Who should look elsewhere

Learners who need accredited certificates or deep technical curricula.

Compare current prices for all platforms in our price and free-trial tracker, or take the platform quiz.

Our take

Use the trial to finish one or two class projects; if you do, the annual plan is reasonable value.
