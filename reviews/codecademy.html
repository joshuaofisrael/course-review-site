Codecademy review (2026): Plus $14.99 a month, Pro $19.99

By the CourseGauge editorial team

Last updated November 21, 2026

Short answer: Codecademy's pricing page on 21 Nov 2026 still listed Basic at $0, Plus at $14.99 a month billed annually or $29.99 billed monthly, and Pro at $19.99 a month billed annually or $39.99 billed monthly. Annual Plus and annual Pro each include one live workshop. Career paths, professional certifications and interview prep are marked Pro only.

Key facts

Pricing modelThree individual tiers on the pricing page, 21 Nov 2026: Basic, Plus and Pro. Team plans are quote only.
Basic$0. Courses are marked Limited. Skill paths, career paths, certification paths, certificates of completion and interview prep are marked not available.
Plus$14.99 a month billed annually ($179.88 for the year) or $29.99 billed monthly. Skill paths are included. Career paths and certification paths are not. Certificates of completion are included. Assessments are marked Limited. Builder credits are marked 3X the Basic amount. One complimentary live workshop on an annual plan only.
Pro$19.99 a month billed annually ($239.88 for the year) or $39.99 billed monthly. Adds career paths, certification paths for AWS, Microsoft, CompTIA, ISC2 and others, technical interview prep, an interview simulator, a job-readiness checker, code challenges, and professional certifications on select career paths. Builder credits are marked 6X. One complimentary workshop on an annual plan only.
Free trialThe pricing page says Try Plus for free and Try Pro for free. It does not print a number of days. The help article that used to state 7 days returned an error on 21 Nov, so the day count is not reconfirmed here.
RefundsThe refund help article also returned an error on 21 Nov. We are not restating a refund rule we could not re-read. Confirm it on Codecademy's help centre before you rely on a cancellation.

Who it suits

A beginner who will use a skill path for most of a year. Plus annual at $179.88 is six cents under six months of the $29.99 monthly plan (29.99 times 6 is $179.94), so the annual plan pays off at month six. Pro is the tier that contains the career path and the interview tools, at $60 more per year than Plus on annual billing (239.88 minus 179.88). The comparison with a data-only library is Codecademy vs DataCamp.

Who should look elsewhere

An experienced developer shopping for one advanced project, and anyone who needs the career path but prices Plus by mistake. The table marks career paths, certification paths and interview prep as absent on Plus. If the goal is maths puzzles rather than writing code, Brilliant is the other browser tool, compared in Brilliant vs Codecademy.

The workshop line is easy to misread. It is not a Pro exclusive. Both annual plans include one complimentary live workshop per new subscription or renewal, with a recording afterwards. A monthly plan does not. Paying Pro prices to "get the workshop" is paying for the career path and the interview tools. The workshop comes along either way if you take the year.

Six months is the break-even

Plus: 14.99 times 12 is $179.88. The monthly plan is $29.99, and six of those are $179.94. From the sixth month, annual Plus has cost less. Pro is the same shape one tier up: 19.99 times 12 is $239.88, and 39.99 times 6 is $239.94. The page did not print a trial length next to "Try Plus for free," and the older help article would not load on 21 Nov, so this review no longer states "7 days" as a checked fact. The refund article would not load either. Use the free Basic tier, which did load, as the way to try the lessons before a card is involved. Our take

Stay on Basic until a skill path is locked. Move to Plus for that path, and to Pro only when the career path or the interview tools are the reason. Annual billing is half the monthly run-rate on both paid tiers: 14.99 times 12 is $179.88 against 29.99 times 12, which is $359.88, and the Pro pair is $239.88 against $479.88. The workshop is an annual-plan extra, not a reason to pick Pro over Plus. Run the numbers in the monthly vs annual calculator.
