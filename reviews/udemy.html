Udemy review (2026): buy one course or subscribe

Short answer: Udemy is the most flexible option: buy a single course and keep lifetime access, or subscribe to the Personal Plan for a curated collection. Refund rules differ sharply between the two.

Key facts

Pricing modelIndividual course purchases, or Personal Plan subscription (monthly or annual access)
Catalog250,000+ courses overall (per Udemy); Personal Plan is a curated subset
Free trialMay be offered to first-time Personal Plan subscribers
RefundsEligible individual courses: 30-day refund policy. Subscriptions bought on Udemy.com: no refunds unless required by law
AccessPurchased courses: lifetime access
CertificatesCertificate of completion (not accredited)

Who it suits

Self-directed learners who want one specific practical skill (a programming language, an exam, a software tool) at a low one-off cost.

Who should look elsewhere

Anyone wanting consistent quality control: courses come from independent instructors, so check reviews and previews.

Our take

For one or two skills, buy courses individually and keep them. Consider the Personal Plan only if you will work through several courses within the subscription period.
