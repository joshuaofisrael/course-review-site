MasterClass review (2026): celebrity-led lessons, annual billing only

Last updated October 19, 2026

Short answer: MasterClass's main membership is best treated as inspiration and craft insight from famous practitioners, not as a credential. Every plan unlocks the full library; you pay more only for extra simultaneous devices and offline viewing. Career-focused certificate courses are sold separately.

Key facts

Pricing modelAnnual membership: Standard $10/mo, Plus $15/mo, Premium $20/mo, billed annually ($120, $180, $240 a year)
Monthly optionAdvertised as billed annually
Free trialNot shown on the plan pages we checked
DevicesStandard 1; Plus up to 2 simultaneous streams; Premium up to 6
CertificatesMain membership: not credential-focused. MasterClass Certificates are a separate paid product: 15–20-hour courses with a capstone project and shareable certificate, from $299 per course or a Certificates Annual Pass
RenewalAuto-renews; renewal notice sent 30 days before
Refunds30-day money-back guarantee (per MasterClass homepage)

Who it suits

Curious learners who enjoy polished, documentary-style lessons and want breadth across cooking, writing, music, business and more. Plus or Premium suits households because of extra streams and profiles.

Who should look elsewhere

Anyone who needs graded work or step-by-step job skills from the main library. The upfront annual charge also makes it a poor fit for trying something for a month. MasterClass Certificates add credentials, but at extra cost (from $299 per course).

Compare current prices for all platforms in our price and free-trial tracker, check whether an annual plan pays off with the monthly vs annual calculator, or take the platform quiz.

Our take

Pick Standard if you watch alone online; Plus if two people share or you want offline downloads. Set a reminder before the renewal date, since the plan auto-renews.
