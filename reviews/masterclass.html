MasterClass review (2026): celebrity-led lessons, annual billing only

Short answer: MasterClass is best treated as inspiration and craft insight from famous practitioners, not as a credential. Every plan unlocks the full library; you pay more only for extra simultaneous devices and offline viewing.

Key facts

Pricing modelAnnual subscription: Standard $120/yr, Plus $180/yr, Premium $240/yr (shown as $10, $15, $20 per month billed annually)
Monthly optionAdvertised as billed annually
Free trialNone listed on the plans we checked
DevicesStandard 1; Plus up to 2 simultaneous streams; Premium up to 6
CertificatesNot a credential-focused platform
RenewalAuto-renews; renewal notice sent 30 days before

Who it suits

Curious learners who enjoy polished, documentary-style lessons and want breadth across cooking, writing, music, business and more. Plus or Premium suits households because of extra streams and profiles.

Who should look elsewhere

Anyone who needs certificates, graded work or step-by-step job skills. The upfront annual charge also makes it a poor fit for trying something for a month.

Compare current prices for all platforms in our price and free-trial tracker, or take the platform quiz.

Our take

Pick Standard if you watch alone online; Plus if two people share or you want offline downloads. Set a reminder before the renewal date, since the plan auto-renews.
