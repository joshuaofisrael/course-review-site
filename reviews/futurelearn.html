FutureLearn review (2026): short university courses, UK roots

Short answer: FutureLearn offers short courses from universities and organisations; Unlimited gives a year of access to most of them.

Key facts

Pricing modelUnlimited annual subscription; individual upgrades also sold
Unlimited price$349.99/year list; $244.99 first-year offer shown on Oct 15, 2026
RenewalAnnual subscription auto-renews
Catalog1,400+ courses (per FutureLearn)

Who it suits

Learners who like short, structured university courses in health, teaching, psychology and business.

Who should look elsewhere

Learners wanting deep technical tracks.
