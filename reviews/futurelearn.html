FutureLearn review (2026): Unlimited is $349.99 a year

By the CourseGauge editorial team

Last updated November 20, 2026

Short answer: FutureLearn Unlimited, on the page we read on 20 Nov 2026, is $349.99 billed annually, shown as $29.17 a month. The $244.99 offer from our October checks was not on that page. Unlimited includes a digital Certificate of Achievement on the short courses it covers, and it excludes microcredentials, degrees and ExpertTrack subscription courses.

Key facts

Pricing modelUnlimited is an annual subscription. The page we checked showed one price, not a separate monthly total. One-off upgrades for single courses still exist, priced on each course.
Unlimited price$349.99 a year, displayed as $29.17 a month, on 20 Nov 2026. No introductory price was printed on the page. The $244.99 figure from October was not there.
RenewalUnlimited renews automatically. FutureLearn says it emails three days before renewal. Cancel before the period ends and you keep access until that period ends.
CatalogThe Unlimited page says 1,400+ short courses from 200+ institutions. Unlimited does not include Paid Upfront courses (microcredentials and degrees), Subscription Access courses inside an ExpertTrack, or program assessments. Open taster courses for degrees are included.
CertificatesA printable digital Certificate of Achievement on included short courses, once you are eligible. If you cancel, you keep those certificates and you keep access to the courses where you earned one. Other courses go back to a free-learner experience. The Unlimited page does not say the certificate is university credit.

Who it suits

Someone who wants a stack of short university courses for a year and cares about keeping the certificate afterwards. At $349.99, Unlimited is $49.01 less than the $399 Coursera Plus annual price from our October read of Coursera. The catalogs are not the same product. FutureLearn's own page puts the count at 1,400+ short courses and names what is left out.

Who should look elsewhere

Learners who need a microcredential, a degree, or an ExpertTrack. Those are outside Unlimited, and buying Unlimited will not unlock them. It is also a weak choice if you wanted the October discount: $244.99 was not on the page on 20 Nov, so budget $349.99 unless the checkout shows something lower that day.

The October discount was not on the page

On 15 Oct and again on 28 Oct, FutureLearn's Unlimited page showed a $349.99 list price and a $244.99 offer. On 20 Nov 2026 the same URL showed one figure: $29.17 a month, $349.99 billed annually. There was no struck-through price and no "30% off" line. We are retiring $244.99 as a current price. If a sale returns, it will be on that page, not in this paragraph.

$29.17 times 12 is $350.04, a few cents over the $349.99 billed total. The page is showing a rounded monthly equivalent of the annual charge, not a $29.17 bill you can pay twelve separate times. We did not see a distinct monthly Unlimited price.

What the $349.99 does not buy

The Unlimited page is explicit about the holes. You get stand-alone short courses that offer a Certificate of Achievement. You do not get Paid Upfront courses, and the page names microcredentials and degrees in that group. You do not get Subscription Access courses that sit inside an ExpertTrack. You do not get program assessments. Degree taster courses that are open to anyone are included. The practical test is the filter labelled Included in Unlimited. A course card without that label is a separate purchase.

The page also says the subscription covers 1,400+ courses and more than 200 institutions. That is FutureLearn's count, from the page, not a catalog we inventoried.

What you keep if you cancel

Renewal is automatic, with an email three days before. Cancel before the period ends and access runs to the end of the period you paid for. Then three things happen, in the page's own split:

  • You keep every Certificate of Achievement you have earned.
  • You keep Unlimited-style access to each course where you earned one.
  • Courses where you did not earn a certificate go back to the free-learner experience.

If you resubscribe later, the page says you regain the courses from the last subscription. That makes Unlimited less of a cliff than a rental that locks the door on every file. It still is not a reason to pay for a year of courses you have not looked at. Coursera Plus at $399 a year is the nearest wide subscription, and FutureLearn vs Coursera puts the certificate rules next to each other. Both prices are in the price tracker.

Our take

Ignore old screenshots of a 30% Unlimited offer unless the live page shows it. The number on 20 Nov was $349.99 for the year. Before you pay, filter the short-course list to "Included in Unlimited" and see whether the courses you want are in that set. After you earn a Certificate of Achievement, cancelling does not take that course away. That is the useful part of the renewal terms, and it is spelled out on the Unlimited page.
