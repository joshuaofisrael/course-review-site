Coursera review (2026): university and industry certificates

Last updated October 19, 2026

Short answer: Coursera is the strongest pick here if you want certificates from universities and companies. Coursera Plus bundles most of the catalog for one price.

Key facts

Pricing modelCoursera Plus $59/month or $399/year; single courses and programs $49–$79/month
Monthly optionYes
Free trialMonthly Plus starts with a 7-day free trial
RefundsAnnual Plus: refund request within 14 days of payment (certificates earned in that window are revoked)
CertificatesYes, certificates for completed courses
Catalog10,000+ courses (per Coursera)
OwnershipCoursera, Inc., which has owned Udemy since May 11, 2026; the two platforms and their accounts still run separately
Free optionFree preview of the first module of most courses (this replaced free auditing in August 2025); some Community Impact courses are free in full; financial aid for eligible learners

Who it suits

Career changers and upskillers who want structured programs, graded assignments and recognisable certificates.

Who should look elsewhere

Hobbyists who want short creative classes, and anyone who will study for only a few weeks at a time and forget to cancel.

Compare current prices for all platforms in our price and free-trial tracker, check whether an annual plan pays off with the monthly vs annual calculator, or take the platform quiz.

Our take

At list prices, seven months of monthly Plus ($413) cost more than one year of annual Plus ($399), so choose annual only if you'll study most of the year. Annual Plus has a 14-day money-back guarantee.
