Coursera review (2026): university and industry certificates

Short answer: Coursera is the strongest pick here if you want certificates from universities and companies. Coursera Plus bundles most of the catalog for one price.

Key facts

Pricing modelCoursera Plus $59/month or $399/year; individual courses and certificates priced separately
Monthly optionYes
Free trialMonthly Plus starts with a 7-day free trial
RefundsAnnual Plus has a 14-day money-back guarantee
CertificatesYes, certificates for completed courses
Catalog10,000+ courses (per Coursera)

Who it suits

Career changers and upskillers who want structured programs, graded assignments and recognisable certificates.

Who should look elsewhere

Hobbyists who want short creative classes, and anyone who will study for only a few weeks at a time and forget to cancel.

Our take

At list prices, roughly seven months of monthly Plus costs more than one year of annual Plus, so commit to annual only if you will study most of the year.
