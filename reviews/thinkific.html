Thinkific review (2026): Start is $109 a month, no free plan

By the CourseGauge editorial team

Last updated November 20, 2026

Short answer: Thinkific is a school platform for people who sell courses, not a catalog you browse. On 20 Nov 2026 its pricing page, last modified 3 Nov 2026, had no free plan. Start is $109 a month, or $82 a month billed annually, after a 30-day trial. Thinkific Payments is listed at 2.9%. Using Stripe instead adds a gateway fee, 2% on Start.

Key facts

Who it is forCreators, academies and companies running their own school. Learners buy from that school. Thinkific does not sell one shared course catalog.
Creator pricing (US list)Start $109/mo or $82/mo billed annually (the page says save $330). Grow $219/mo or $164/mo billed annually (save $660). Expand $499/mo or $374/mo billed annually (save $1,500). Plus is a sales quote. A branded mobile app add-on is $199/mo, included on select Plus plans. The annual toggle is labelled save 25%.
Free trial30 days on the whole platform. The pricing FAQ says there is no free plan. You can cancel during the trial.
Creator refundsThe pricing FAQ says you can cancel Start, Grow or Expand at any time, including during the 30-day trial. It does not state a money-back guarantee after the trial ends.
Transaction feesThinkific Payments, the built-in processor, is described as 2.9%, with no extra gateway fee. Stripe and PayPal are also supported. Thinkific's Stripe help article charges an extra gateway fee on third-party processing: 2% on Start, 1% on Grow, 0.5% on Expand, and none on Plus, for the first $1 million of sales in a calendar year. The fee table still names Free and Basic plans. The public pricing page no longer sells those. It offers a 30-day trial instead.
Product limitsStart, Grow and Expand each list 10,000 current students. Start includes unlimited courses, 1 community, 200 GB of video a month, groups of 50 and 2 admin seats. Grow raises that to 3 communities, 400 GB, groups of 100 and 6 admins, and removes Thinkific branding. Expand goes to 10 communities, 1,000 GB, unlimited group size and 15 admins.
Certificates for learnersStart includes quizzes, assignments and branded certificates. Whether a learner receives one still depends on how that school sets the course up.
For learnersPrice, access length and refunds are set by the school, not by one Thinkific learner policy.

Who it suits

A creator who will publish more than a handful of courses and wants certificates, a custom domain and communities on the entry paid plan. Start does not meter you at 5 or 10 products the way Teachable Starter and Builder do. Annual billing is the number to use if the school will still be on Thinkific in month eight: Grow's displayed annual saving, $660, matches 12 times ($219 minus $164).

Who should look elsewhere

A learner who wanted one subscription that covers many teachers. You are buying from one school. Also a poor fit if you are testing a first course and might quit in month two. There is no free plan, Start is $109 a month, and the pricing FAQ does not offer a refund once the 30-day trial is over. Teachable Starter is $39 a month, with a 7.5% fee on sales. Run the example in Teachable vs Thinkific before you assume the higher plan is the expensive one.

What the pricing page actually lists

Thinkific's pricing page was read on 20 Nov 2026. The page's own schema says it was modified on 3 Nov 2026. The plans on offer are Start, Grow, Expand and a quote-only Plus. The FAQ on that page answers "Does Thinkific have a free plan?" with no. The substitute is a 30-day trial of the whole platform, and the same FAQ says you can cancel during the trial.

Thinkific list prices on the pricing page, 20 Nov 2026

The branded iOS and Android app is not in those prices. It is an add-on at $199 a month, and the page says it is included on select Plus plans. That is a different product from the Teachable student apps listed on Teachable Starter.

Where the fee sits

The pricing FAQ says Thinkific Payments, the built-in processor, is priced in line with Stripe and PayPal at 2.9%, and that customers on Thinkific Payments do not pay a third-party gateway fee. The same FAQ says Stripe and PayPal still work, and that an extra gateway fee then applies and varies by plan. The dollar detail is in the Stripe help article, not on the pricing page:

  • Start: 2% of the sale, on top of Stripe's own processing
  • Grow: 1%
  • Expand: 0.5%
  • Plus: no gateway fee at this time

The article limits that fee to the first $1 million of sales in a calendar year, and it applies to creators in the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom from 3 July 2024, and in the EU from 6 Nov 2024. The table still has columns for Free and Basic. Those names are not on the current pricing page. If you are choosing Start, Grow, Expand or Plus, use the rows above. If a checkout still shows Basic, stop and read the fee line before you assume it is 2%.

Payouts are not one number. The pricing FAQ says that with the built-in processing "you receive instant access to your funds in your account." That is Thinkific's sentence, not a bank-deposit clock we timed. The Stripe article says Stripe sales land in your Stripe account, and Stripe's own payout schedule is what sends them to the bank. You can change that schedule in Stripe.

Our take

Count the sales, not the sticker. On Thinkific Payments, a Start plan plus 2.9% on $1,000 of course sales is $109 plus $29, or $138, before any tax tool. Teachable Starter on the same $1,000 is $39 plus a 7.5% transaction fee ($75), or $114, and the card processor's own cut is extra on both. Teachable Builder at $89 with a 0% Teachable fee beats Starter once the 7.5% exceeds the $50 plan gap, which is about $667 of sales in a month. Confirm Start's annual invoice at checkout: the page shows $82 a month and a "Save $330" label, while 12 times ($109 minus $82) is $324.
