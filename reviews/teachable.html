Teachable review (2026): platform for selling your own course

Short answer: Teachable is a platform for creators to sell courses. For learners, courses hosted on Teachable are run by independent creators, so quality and refund terms vary by school.

Key facts

Pricing modelCreator plans: Starter, Builder, Growth, Custom; monthly or annual
FeesTeachable lists a platform transaction fee on Starter only; payment processing fees apply
For learnersEach school sets its own prices and refund policy

Who it suits

Creators who want to sell courses under their own brand.

Who should look elsewhere

Learners wanting a curated catalog.

Our take

Learners: check each school's refund policy before buying.
