Online course platform price and free-trial tracker

Short answer: the cheapest ways to learn are fully free options (Khan Academy, Alison, edX audit, Duolingo's free tier). Among paid subscriptions, annual billing is almost always cheaper per month than monthly billing. Watch renewal prices and refund windows. Prices below are US list prices taken from each platform's official pages.

Method

Each figure was read on the platform's own pricing page, plan page or help centre on the date shown. Where we could not confirm a figure on an official page, we show "see official pricing" with a link instead of estimating. Promotional prices are labelled. Prices vary by region, taxes and app-store billing. A script re-fetches every source and flags changes for us to review, so we re-check pages whenever they change.
