Online course platform price and free-trial tracker

Short answer: the cheapest ways to learn are fully free options (Khan Academy, Alison, edX audit, Duolingo's free tier). Among paid subscriptions, annual billing is almost always cheaper per month than monthly billing. Watch renewal prices and refund windows. Prices below are US list prices taken from each platform's official pages.

Method

Each figure was read on the platform's own pricing page, plan page or help centre on the date shown. Where we could not confirm a figure on an official page, we show "see official pricing" with a link instead of estimating. Promotional prices are labelled. Prices vary by region, taxes and app-store billing. A script re-fetches every source and flags changes for us to review, so we re-check pages whenever they change.

Frequently asked questions

Which online course platforms are completely free?

Khan Academy is free in full. Alison courses are free to study and complete, with paid certificates optional. edX lets you audit many courses for free (no graded work or certificate), and Duolingo, Codecademy (Basic) and Brilliant have free plans. Coursera no longer offers free auditing: since August 2025 it gives a free preview of the first module of most courses. See our guide to free course platforms.

Which course platforms offer a free trial?

In our October 15, 2026 check: Coursera Plus, Skillshare, Codecademy (card required), The Great Courses Plus (new customers) and Teachable offer 7 days, Duolingo offers 1 week, and Pluralsight offers 10 days (excluding labs and sandboxes). Trial offers change often, so check the linked official page before signing up.

Which platforms give refunds?

MasterClass and Teachable advertise a 30-day money-back guarantee, and Coursera Plus annual has a 14-day money-back guarantee. Pluralsight individual plans, Codecademy subscriptions and DataCamp fees are generally non-refundable, so use the free trial or free tier first.

Is it cheaper to pay monthly or annually?

Annual billing is usually cheaper per month, but only pays off if you keep learning long enough. Our monthly vs annual calculator shows the break-even month for each plan.
