Frequently asked questions

How do I choose an online course platform?

Start with your goal: certificates for a career (Coursera, edX), practical skills from individual courses (Udemy), creative classes (Skillshare, MasterClass) or languages (Duolingo). Then compare price, free trial and refund terms. Our 5-question quiz narrows it down.

Is a subscription or a one-off course purchase better value?

It depends on how much you will use it. Subscriptions suit regular learners, while buying single courses suits a specific skill. Our monthly vs annual calculator shows how many months of use make an annual plan cheaper.

Which platforms offer a free trial?

Trial terms vary by platform and change often. Our price and free-trial tracker lists current trials and prices for 20 platforms, each linked to the platform's own pages.

Do online course certificates count for anything?

Certificates of completion show you finished a course but are not accredited degrees. Some university-backed certificates on platforms such as Coursera and edX carry more weight with employers. Each review notes what certificate a platform offers.

How does CourseGauge research its reviews?

Every price, trial and refund fact is linked to the platform's own pages or a named source, and pages show when they were last checked. CourseGauge has no affiliate relationships and earns no commission. See how we research.
