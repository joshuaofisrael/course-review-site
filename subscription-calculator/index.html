Monthly or annual? Online course subscription break-even calculator

Short answer: on the 11 popular plans below, an annual subscription costs the same or less than paying monthly once you would use it for 5 to 9 months in a year (median 7 months). If you expect to study for less than about 5 months, pay monthly and cancel; if you will learn most of the year, annual billing saves about 29% to 60% over a full year.

What the numbers show

  • The quickest payback is on discounted annual offers: DataCamp's special annual price and Domestika's first-year price both pay off within 5 months, but both are promotional and Domestika renews at $329.
  • Codecademy and Babbel price annual billing at about half the monthly rate, so annual wins from 6 months of use.
  • Coursera Plus at $399 a year beats $59 a month from month 7.
  • Brilliant, Rosetta Stone and Pluralsight discount annual plans less (about 29% to 34% over a year), so you need 8 or 9 months of use before annual is the better deal.

Before you pay for a year up front

  • Check the refund terms. Some annual plans are non-refundable once the trial ends (for example Pluralsight individual plans), while Coursera Plus annual has a 14-day money-back guarantee. See every policy in the price and free-trial tracker.
  • Use the free trial to test your habit. If you are not using a trial most days, the monthly plan, or a free option, is probably the safer bet. Our guide to free course platforms covers the no-cost alternatives.
  • Watch renewal prices. Introductory annual prices can jump at renewal. Set a reminder a week before the renewal date.
  • Prices vary. These are US list prices. Taxes, app-store billing and regional pricing change the totals, so confirm on the official page before buying.

Method

Monthly price and annual total come from each platform's official pricing page on October 15, 2026 (the same figures as our price tracker). Where a platform shows annual billing as a per-month figure, we multiplied it by 12. "Annual pays off from" is the smallest whole number of months for which paying monthly costs at least as much as one year of annual billing. Platforms that only sell annual memberships (such as MasterClass) or whose US prices we could not confirm are left out rather than estimated. The calculator runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing.

Frequently asked questions

Is an annual online course subscription worth it?

Only if you will keep learning most of the year. On the 11 plans we compared, annual billing costs the same or less than monthly once you use it for 5 to 9 months in a year (median 7 months). Below that, paying monthly and cancelling is cheaper.

How do I work out the break-even month myself?

Divide the annual price by the monthly price and round up. For Coursera Plus, $399 a year divided by $59 a month is about 6.8, so annual is cheaper from month 7.

Can I get a refund on an annual plan?

It depends on the platform. Coursera Plus annual has a 14-day money-back guarantee, while Pluralsight individual plans are not refundable. Check every policy in our price and free-trial tracker before paying for a year.

Do introductory annual prices last?

Not always. Domestika Plus costs $164.50 in the first year but renews at $329, which moves break-even from 5 months to 10. DataCamp's annual figure is also a special price, so check the renewal price before you buy.
