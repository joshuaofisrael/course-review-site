MasterClass vs Coursera vs Skillshare vs Udemy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Short answer: Choose Coursera for certificates, Udemy for one specific skill at a one-off price, Skillshare for hands-on creative projects, and MasterClass for inspiration from famous practitioners.

Cheapest way to start

Udemy, because you can buy a single course rather than subscribe. Coursera's monthly Plus has a 7-day free trial, and Skillshare offers a trial too.

Best for a career move

Coursera, thanks to university and industry certificates and graded work.

Best for creatives

Skillshare for short project classes; MasterClass for craft insight rather than step-by-step practice.

Frequently asked questions

Which of MasterClass, Coursera, Skillshare and Udemy has a free trial?

Coursera's monthly Plus plan has a 7-day free trial. Skillshare offers a 7-day trial, after which the annual membership is billed in full. Udemy offers Personal Plan trials through promotions for some new subscribers. We did not see a free trial on MasterClass's plan pages, but it advertises a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Which has the best refund policy?

On paper, MasterClass: a 30-day money-back guarantee on membership. Udemy also gives 30 days on eligible individual courses, but not on subscriptions bought on Udemy.com. Coursera refunds annual Plus if you ask within 14 days of paying. Skillshare is strictest: after a 7-day trial, a one-time refund only if you ask within 48 hours of the charge and haven't used it since, and no refunds on renewals (EU and UK users get a 14-day cooling-off period).

How much does each one cost per year?

MasterClass: $120, $180 or $240 a year (Standard, Plus, Premium). Coursera Plus: $399 a year, or $59 a month. Skillshare: $167.88 a year on the web (shown as $13.99 a month). Udemy: buy single courses, or the Personal Plan from $13.00 a month billed monthly or annually. See all of them in the price and free-trial tracker.

Do MasterClass and Skillshare give certificates?

Not as part of their main memberships in a career sense. MasterClass now sells separate certificate courses (from $299 per course) with a capstone project. Skillshare's pricing page mentions certificates and badges to mark progress but doesn't present them as accredited credentials. For career certificates, see best platforms for certificates.

Are Coursera and Udemy owned by the same company?

Yes. Coursera completed its combination with Udemy on May 11, 2026, but the two still run as separate platforms with separate accounts and subscriptions. More in Coursera vs Udemy.
