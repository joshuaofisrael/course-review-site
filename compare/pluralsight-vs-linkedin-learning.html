Frequently asked questions

Should I choose Pluralsight or LinkedIn Learning?

Choose Pluralsight if you work in tech or want to: it focuses on software development, IT operations, cloud, data, AI and security, with Skill IQ assessments, learning paths, certification prep and hands-on labs, plus cloud and AI sandboxes on the Cloud+, AI+ and Complete plans. Choose LinkedIn Learning for broader business and software skills, especially if you would also use LinkedIn Premium or can get access through your employer or school. Our best platforms to learn coding and tech guide compares more options.

Can I buy LinkedIn Learning on its own?

No. LinkedIn's help center says it does not sell individual LinkedIn Learning subscriptions or single courses. Individuals get it through LinkedIn Premium, and organisations such as businesses, universities and government agencies can buy it for their people. If your employer or school already has a licence, you may not need to pay at all, so check that first. Pluralsight, by contrast, sells individual plans directly.

How much do Pluralsight and LinkedIn Learning cost?

At our October 15, 2026 check, Pluralsight cost $21 a month for Core Tech, $24.50 a month for AI+, Cloud+, Data+ or Security+, and $39 a month for Complete, all billed yearly, or $30 (Core Tech) and $55 (Complete) billed monthly. Pluralsight's pricing page loads its prices with JavaScript and we could not reconfirm them on October 28, so check before you buy. For LinkedIn Learning you pay for a LinkedIn Premium plan; we haven't confirmed current Premium prices on a public official LinkedIn page, so check LinkedIn's Premium page before you subscribe. See the price tracker and the monthly vs annual calculator.

Do Pluralsight and LinkedIn Learning have free trials?

Yes. Pluralsight offers a 10-day free trial on any individual plan. It includes your plan's full course library, Skill IQ, certification prep, learning paths and a selection of hands-on labs; offline downloads and the full labs and sandboxes need a paid plan. If you don't cancel, the trial turns into a paid subscription on day 11. LinkedIn Premium's free trial lasts one month for eligible users, and billing starts automatically when it ends. Set a reminder either way.

Can I get a refund, and do they give certificates?

Pluralsight says it doesn't refund individual subscriptions because you can cancel at any time; you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for, and it doesn't send renewal reminder emails. We haven't verified LinkedIn Premium's refund terms on an official page, so cancel before your trial ends if you're unsure. Both give certificates of completion, and LinkedIn Learning lets you add completed courses to your LinkedIn profile. More detail is in our Pluralsight review and LinkedIn Learning review.
