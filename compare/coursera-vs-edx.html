Coursera vs edX

Last updated October 25, 2026

Short answer: Both offer university content. edX's free audit track is good for sampling; Coursera Plus is better value if you'll take many courses.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take Coursera or edX courses for free?

edX still lets you audit many courses free, with temporary access to the course materials but no graded assignments or certificate. Coursera ended free auditing in August 2025; it now offers a free preview of the first module of most courses, some Community Impact courses free in full, and financial aid for eligible learners. For fully free options, see our best free online course platforms.

Is Coursera or edX cheaper?

It depends on how much you study. edX prices each course separately, so one certificate can cost less than months of a subscription; check the course's enrollment options for its price. Coursera charges $49–$79 a month for single courses and programs, or $59 a month or $399 a year for Coursera Plus, which covers most of its catalog. If you plan several courses in a year, Coursera Plus often works out cheaper. Until November 4, 2026, edX is also taking 15% off select verified courses and programs (details of the edX fall offer).

Do Coursera and edX give certificates?

Yes. Coursera gives a certificate when you complete a course on a paid plan or with financial aid. On edX you get a verified certificate only on the paid verified track, and you need to upgrade before the course's upgrade deadline. Neither is a degree, so check how an employer views a certificate before you pay for it.

Does Coursera or edX have a free trial?

Coursera's monthly Plus plan starts with a 7-day free trial. edX has no trial, because the free audit track already lets you try most courses before you upgrade.

Which is better for university courses, Coursera or edX?

Both carry courses from universities and companies. edX suits learners who want to audit academic material free and pay only if they want the certificate. Coursera suits learners who expect to take several courses and want one subscription; its first-module preview lets you sample before paying. Our platform quiz can help you choose.
