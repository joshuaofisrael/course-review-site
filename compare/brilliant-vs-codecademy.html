Brilliant vs Codecademy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Short answer: Pick Brilliant for short daily quantitative problem-solving across math, science and CS concepts. Pick Codecademy when your goal is learning to write code in a guided browser IDE, especially with career paths on paid plans.

Learning style

Brilliant emphasises interactive problem solving rather than long video lectures. Codecademy has you write code in the browser from early lessons, with paid tiers for career paths and certifications.

Pricing snapshot

Brilliant Premium lists individual plans at $30/month or $20/month billed annually, plus family plans, with a free plan that includes a daily lesson. Codecademy offers a free basic tier; Plus and Pro are paid (Plus $14.99/month billed annually or $29.99 monthly; Pro $19.99/month billed annually or $39.99 monthly, per its pricing page on 5 Nov 2026), and its pricing page offers a free trial of Plus; check the trial length and card terms at signup.

Certificates

Brilliant is not credential-focused. Codecademy's Pro tier adds career paths, interview prep and certifications per its pricing page.

Frequently asked questions

Is Brilliant free?

Brilliant offers a free plan with a daily lesson. Full unlimited access requires Premium. K-12 teachers and students may qualify for free Premium per Brilliant's help pages.

Does Codecademy have a free tier?

Yes. Codecademy's basic tier is free; Plus and Pro are paid upgrades.

Which is better for learning Python?

Codecademy, if you want a guided coding path in the browser. Brilliant can build CS and math intuition but is not a full professional coding curriculum.

Which has a free trial?

Codecademy's pricing page offers a free trial of Plus; check the length and card terms at signup. Brilliant's free plan is the main no-cost entry; check Brilliant's subscribe pages for any current Premium trial offers.
