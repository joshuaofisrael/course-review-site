Coursera vs Udemy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Short answer: Coursera for recognised certificates and structured programs; Udemy for cheap, practical single courses with lifetime access.

Same owner, separate platforms

Coursera completed its combination with Udemy on May 11, 2026, so Udemy is now a wholly owned subsidiary of Coursera, Inc. For learners nothing has merged yet: Coursera says the platforms, accounts and subscriptions stay separate for now, and a Coursera Plus subscription does not include Udemy courses. We compare them as two different products, because that is how you buy them today.

Refunds

Udemy individual courses have a 30-day refund policy, but Udemy.com subscriptions do not. Coursera annual Plus has a 14-day money-back guarantee.

Frequently asked questions

Are Coursera and Udemy the same company now?

Yes, but they are still separate platforms. Coursera completed its combination with Udemy on May 11, 2026, and Udemy is now owned by Coursera, Inc. Coursera says accounts, subscriptions and catalogs remain separate for now: Coursera Plus does not include Udemy courses, and you cannot merge a Coursera and a Udemy account.

Is Coursera or Udemy better for certificates?

Coursera. Its certificates come from courses built with university and company partners, and Coursera Plus includes unlimited certificates once the trial ends. Udemy gives a certificate of completion that is not accredited. If a certificate matters for a job, check how that employer views it before you pay. See our best platforms for certificates.

Which is cheaper, Coursera or Udemy?

It depends on how many courses you take. For one specific skill, Udemy is usually the lower commitment: you pay once per course, keep lifetime access, and eligible courses have a 30-day refund window. Udemy's Personal Plan starts at $13.00 a month. Coursera charges $49–$79 a month for single courses and programs, or $59 a month or $399 a year for Coursera Plus. If you will study on Coursera for 7 or more months in a year, annual Plus is cheaper than monthly; check with our monthly vs annual calculator.

Do Coursera and Udemy offer free trials?

Coursera's monthly Plus plan starts with a 7-day free trial; Coursera takes your payment details but charges nothing if you cancel during the trial. Udemy offers Personal Plan free trials through promotions for some new subscribers, and you can cancel during the trial without being charged.

Can I get a refund from Coursera or Udemy?

Udemy refunds eligible individual courses within 30 days, but it can refuse refunds after heavy use or repeated requests, and some refunds are paid as Udemy credits. Udemy subscriptions bought on Udemy.com are not refundable unless local law requires it. Coursera's annual Plus plan can be refunded if you ask within 14 days of paying, though any certificates earned in that window are revoked.

Can I still audit Coursera courses for free?

Not in the old way. In August 2025 Coursera replaced free auditing with a free preview of the first module of most courses, including graded assignments where the course has them. Some Community Impact courses are free in full, and eligible learners can apply for financial aid. For fully free options, see our best free online course platforms.
