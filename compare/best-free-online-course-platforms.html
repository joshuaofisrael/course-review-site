Frequently asked questions

What is the best completely free online learning platform?

Khan Academy. It is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and its whole library of videos, articles, practice questions and lessons is free, with no ads. It is strongest for school and early-college subjects. Its AI tutor, Khanmigo, is a separate product that is paid for learners where offered.

Can I get a free certificate from an online course?

Sometimes, but usually not. Alison lets you study and complete courses free and download a free Learner Record; printed or digital certificates are optional purchases, though some courses include a free digital one. edX's free audit track gives no certificate, and Coursera certificates need a paid plan or financial aid.

Is edX free?

The audit track is free. It gives you videos, readings, ungraded practice and discussion forums for a limited time, counted from when you enrol. Graded assignments and exams, the verified certificate, and access after the course ends all need the paid verified track, which is priced per course. See our edX review.

Is Coursera free?

Partly. Since August 2025 Coursera has offered a free preview of the first module of most courses instead of free auditing. Some Community Impact courses are free in full, and financial aid can cover full access for eligible learners. Otherwise you pay per course or program ($49–$79 a month) or subscribe to Coursera Plus; compare it in Coursera vs Udemy.

Is Duolingo really free?

Yes. All Duolingo course content is free, with ads and some limits. Super Duolingo removes ads and adds unlimited hearts and extra practice, with a 1-week free trial. Duolingo's terms say payments are nonrefundable unless the law requires otherwise. See best language learning apps.
