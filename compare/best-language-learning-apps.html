Frequently asked questions

Is Duolingo really free?

Yes. Duolingo says it offers the same lesson content to every learner, so you can work through full courses free, with ads and some limits. Super Duolingo removes ads and adds unlimited hearts, personalised practice and unlimited attempts at Legendary challenges, with a 1-week free trial. See our Duolingo review.

How much do Babbel and Rosetta Stone cost?

On 20 Nov 2026 Babbel's review page listed $80.70 for 6 months, $107.40 for 12 months and $299.99 lifetime. Shorter-plan totals were not printed there. Rosetta Stone's plans page the same day showed $19.99 a month or $159 a year, for all 25 languages. The price tracker has the check date on each row.

Is a lifetime language app plan worth it?

Only if you are sure you will keep using the same app for years. Babbel's lifetime plan costs about the same as 2.8 years of its 12-month plan at the prices above. Rosetta Stone's lifetime access lasts while the product and services remain available and supported, so read its terms first. Our monthly vs annual calculator helps with the shorter plans.

Do language learning apps give certificates?

Duolingo, Babbel and Rosetta Stone do not give accredited certificates for their courses. The Duolingo English Test is a separate, paid product. If you need a certificate, see our best platforms for certificates.

Which language app should a beginner start with?

Start with Duolingo's free tier to build a daily habit. Move to Babbel if you want structured lessons built around practical conversation and grammar, or Rosetta Stone if you prefer immersion and want access to many languages. Our Babbel vs Duolingo comparison goes into more detail.
