Frequently asked questions

Can I learn to code for free?

You can start free. Codecademy has a free basic tier, and DataCamp's free Basic tier includes the first chapter of every course. Udemy sells single courses with lifetime access, which can be cheaper than a subscription if you only need one skill. For more free options, see our best free online course platforms.

Which coding platforms have a free trial?

Codecademy gives new accounts a 7-day free trial of Plus or Pro; a card is required and you are charged after day 7. Pluralsight offers a 10-day trial that excludes labs and sandboxes. Udemy's Personal Plan may offer a trial to first-time subscribers. Udacity relies on personalised offers and sales rather than a standard trial.

How much do coding platforms cost?

At our last checks, Codecademy Pro cost $19.99 a month billed annually or $39.99 billed monthly, DataCamp Premium $35 a month billed monthly, and Pluralsight Core Tech $21 a month billed yearly or $30 billed monthly. Udacity All Access cost $249 a month or $846 for four months upfront, and Udemy's Personal Plan started at $13.00 a month. The price tracker shows the check date for each figure.

Do coding platform certificates help you get a job?

They show you finished a course or path but are not degrees. Codecademy Pro includes certifications and interview prep, DataCamp Premium includes certificates and certifications, Pluralsight includes certification prep, and Udacity gives Nanodegree program certificates. Udemy's certificate of completion is not accredited. Employers usually care more about projects you can show, so build a portfolio alongside any certificate.

Can I get a refund on a coding subscription?

Usually not. Codecademy and Pluralsight do not refund individual subscriptions, and DataCamp's terms say fees are non-refundable except as stated. Udemy is the exception for single courses: eligible purchases have a 30-day refund policy. Check each platform review for the details and sources, and compare Codecademy vs DataCamp if you are choosing between the two.
